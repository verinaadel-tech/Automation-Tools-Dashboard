Review Escalation Types — Jun 22–26

Key Business Signals Requires attention
🚨
Uganda — Fake Product Crisis
Fake Product claims account for 65–71% of all UG escalations every single day this week (UG29–UG33). This is a systematic pattern — not a one-off. Immediate Trust & Safety investigation of the Uganda seller base is required to prevent GMV loss and customer trust damage.
📦
Morocco — 88.9% QC Rejection Rate
Nearly 9 in 10 Morocco product submissions were rejected in the Jun 25 QC session (2,588 out of 2,910). Primary driver: missing or incorrect color attributes. Suggests a seller onboarding / training gap specific to MA that is blocking listings from going live.
🔧
Defective Product — Top Escalation Type
809 defective product escalations (49.4% of all escalations) across NG, GH, and KE. These are customers explicitly flagging broken or non-functional items received. A seller quality review across these three markets is recommended.
⚡
Automation Delivering Clear ROI
The QC tool saved 556 hours of manual effort in a single session — equivalent to 23 working days. At 94.3% automation rate, only 47 products required human review out of 11,414 processed. The Review Agent processes 370 reviews/second.
